Play the games on your RomM server.

RomP is a free app for Mac, Windows and Linux. Choose a game and press Play. RomP gets the emulator for you.

Download

macOS, Windows, Linux

Press Play

RomP gets the emulator and the BIOS files for you. Games you download play offline.

The screen you remember

Each console starts with the screen it was played on. An old TV, a Game Boy screen, or sharp pixels.

RetroAchievements

Earn achievements as you play

Sign in with your RetroAchievements account. You get popups, leaderboards and hardcore mode. Each game's page shows what you earned.

Save anywhere

Each save state has a picture of the moment. Saves sync with RomM, so you can continue on another computer.

Two screens for DS

Each screen gets its own window. The mouse is the pen.

Also

  • Every game runs in its own sandbox. A bad emulator or ROM can't touch your other files.
  • Interface sizes of 1x, 1.5x or 2x.
  • Gamepads, light guns, mice and keyboards.
  • A game menu on the Guide button or Esc.
  • If a save changed in two places, you choose which one to keep.
  • The whole app works with a controller.
