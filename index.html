Romp
GitHub Download

Play the games on your RomM server.

Romp is a free app for Mac, Windows and Linux. Choose a game and press Play. Romp gets the emulator for you.

Download

macOS, Windows, Linux

Press Play

Romp downloads the emulator and the BIOS files. Your saves sync with RomM, so you can continue on another computer.

Two screens for DS

Each screen gets its own window. The mouse is the pen.

Also

  • Every game runs in its own sandbox. A bad emulator or ROM can't touch your other files.
  • RetroAchievements, with hardcore mode.
  • Gamepads, light guns, mice and keyboards.
  • Old TV and handheld screen looks for every console.
  • Save states with a picture of the moment.
  • Downloaded games play offline.

Download Romp

Free and open source. You need a RomM server, version 5.0 or newer.
